MERA cleaning / processing summary

Dataset:
- Source: MERA-evaluation/MERA.
- Goal: add Russian SFT data aligned with our MCQ / numeric boxed-answer format.
- We processed only useful Russian tasks and saved all clean usable rows; caps will be applied later in the SFT builder, not in the notebook.

Selected subsets:
- Included:
  - mamuramu
  - mathlogicqa
  - ruopenbookqa
  - ruworldtree
  - parus
- Excluded:
  - chegeka: inspected manually and excluded because it is open-answer quiz QA, not MCQ/numeric.
  - multiq: skipped because it is open-answer extractive QA.
  - rumultiar/simplear: skipped for now.
  - rcb
  - rwsd
  - use
  - all diagnostic/safety/code/algorithmic tasks not aligned with multilingual MCQ SFT.
  - all test/public_test splits.

Split policy:
- We used only train, validation, and dev splits.
- validation/dev were mapped to internal val.
- test and public_test were skipped to avoid benchmark leakage.
- Final splits:
  - mamuramu: train 285
  - mathlogicqa: train 680
  - parus: train 400, val 100
  - rcb: train 438, val 220
  - ruopenbookqa: train 2338
  - ruworldtree: train 115
  - rwsd: train 606, val 204
  - use: train 1909, val 660

Task-format decisions:
- Kept only task types compatible with our SFT format:
  - mcq
  - numeric
- Removed all short_answer rows.
- This excluded CheGeKa entirely and removed the open-text USE tasks.
- Final task-type distribution:
  - mcq: 5386 rows
  - numeric: 2569 rows
  - total: 7955 rows

RWSD reformatting:
- RWSD originally had binary answers Да / Нет.
- Distribution was balanced enough:
  - total: Да 388, Нет 422
  - train: Да 297, Нет 309
  - val: Да 91, Нет 113
- We reformatted RWSD into standard A/B MCQ:
  - A) Да
  - B) Нет
- Answers were mapped as:
  - Да -> A
  - Нет -> B
- Metadata keeps the raw answer.

USE filtering:
- USE originally had multiple task types: multiple_choice, matching, and text.
- We kept only multiple-choice/matching-style tasks and classified them as numeric because answers are often numeric sequences such as "1,4" or "8,1,9,7".
- We removed open-text USE tasks.
- Final USE count after filtering: 2569 rows.

Final saved files:
- multilingual/data/processed/mera/mera_clean_all.csv
- multilingual/data/processed/mera/mera_excluded_short_answer.csv
- multilingual/data/processed/mera/mera_rejected_all.csv
- Also saved per-subset files:
  - mera_clean_mamuramu.csv
  - mera_clean_mathlogicqa.csv
  - mera_clean_parus.csv
  - mera_clean_ruopenbookqa.csv
  - mera_clean_ruworldtree.csv

Final subset distribution:
- mamuramu: 285 mcq
- mathlogicqa: 680 mcq
- parus: 500 mcq
- ruopenbookqa: 2338 mcq
- ruworldtree: 115 mcq

Notes for caps later:
- Do not cap in the notebook; cap in the SFT builder.
- ruopenbookqa and use dominate MERA, so they may need moderate caps.
- mamuramu and ruworldtree are small enough to keep fully.
- parus are useful structured reasoning tasks and can be moderately capped or kept depending on the total Russian budget.
